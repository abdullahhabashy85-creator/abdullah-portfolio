Analysis

Key Insights and Recommendations

Key Insights

  • Total sales revenue reached $57.88M across 4,700 vehicles sold.
  • SUVs ($31.65M) and Sedans ($21.45M) represent over 91% of total revenue.
  • Mercedes-Benz led brand revenue with $9.8M, followed by BMW ($8.6M) and Audi ($8.6M).
  • Automatic transmissions overwhelmingly dominate customer demand at 84.88% ($49.13M) of total revenue.
  • Cars with an accident history experienced a severe price drop averaging $9.27K compared to clean vehicles.
  • Vehicles aged 1–5 years generate peak revenue ($8.3M to $6.0M), followed by a sharp depreciation drop after 10 years.
  • Georgia ($5.9M), California ($5.3M), and Texas ($5.3M) are the highest revenue-generating states.
  • 1,606 vehicles had a Full Service history, maintaining a competitive market valuation.

Recommendations

  • Allocate over 80% of purchasing budget toward Automatic SUVs and Sedans.
  • Replace flat price cuts on accident vehicles with a multi-tier inspection score to recover higher resale margins.
  • Launch a "Certified Pre-Owned" (CPO) badge for vehicles with Full Service history to justify premium pricing.
  • Accelerate inventory turnover for vehicles older than 10 years through targeted promotional campaigns.
  • Prioritize inventory allocation and localized marketing in top states (GA, CA, TX).
  • Enforce mandatory state tracking during intake to reduce unclassified location records.
  • Balance luxury inventory (Mercedes/BMW) for high margins with budget brands (Hyundai) for fast cash flow.
